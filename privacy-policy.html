Arrowlet: Arrow Puzzle — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026

This policy explains what information the Arrowlet mobile game ("the app") handles. The app is published by Jay Thummar ("we"). Questions: jppatel10125@gmail.com.

Information the game stores

Your level, settings, streaks, medals, achievements and hints are saved only on your device, in the app's local storage. We do not run servers for the game, do not require an account, and do not receive this data.

Advertising

The app is free and shows ads served by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google's Mobile Ads SDK automatically collects and shares with Google:

  • your IP address, which may be used to estimate your approximate location;
  • interactions with ads and the app (for example app launches, taps and video views);
  • diagnostic information (for example app launch time and performance);
  • device identifiers, including the Android advertising ID and app set ID.

This data is encrypted in transit. Google processes it under its own policies: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the app asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown, and you can change your choice later. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your device's Google settings.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Ads are limited to content suitable for general audiences.

Deleting your data

Use Settings › Reset progress in the app, or uninstall the app, to delete everything it has stored on your device. For data held by Google for advertising, use your device's Google ad settings or contact Google.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
