Arrowlet

Arrow Puzzle for Android

Every board is a tangle of arrows. Tap one and it slides along its own path and off the board — but only if nothing is in its way. Find the right order and clear the board.

Endless levels that grow step by step, a fresh Daily Challenge with monthly medals, leagues, streaks and achievements. No timers, no pressure.
